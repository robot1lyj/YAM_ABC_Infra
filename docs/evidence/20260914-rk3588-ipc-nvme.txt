## NVMe initialization and persistent mount

- After the user explicitly authorized initialization, `/dev/nvme0n1` was given a GPT partition table and one full-size partition `/dev/nvme0n1p1`.
- `/dev/nvme0n1p1` was formatted ext4 with label `yam-data`; filesystem UUID: `501fb615-1346-455d-9d50-61c1d113faf5`.

- Root-privileged `findmnt --verify --verbose` reported no errors or warnings for the entry. The `nofail` option prevents a missing NVMe from blocking boot.
